How to Use the Test Platform

{% if is_student %} ← Back to My Tests {% else %} ← Back to Login {% endif %}

1. Logging In

Use the Roll Number and Password your teacher gave you. This one login works for every subject's tests — you don't need a separate account per subject.

2. Finding a Test

"My Tests" lists every test open to your batch. As more tests get added over the year, use:

  • Search — type part of a test's name or chapter to jump straight to it.
  • Filter by Chapter — pick a chapter/unit to see only tests for what you're currently studying.

3. Your Progress

The top of "My Tests" shows how many tests and chapters you've attempted so far, plus a chapter-by-chapter breakdown — a quick way to spot chapters you haven't practiced yet.

4. Taking a Test

Click Start on any active test. If it has a timer, keep the page open — closing or refreshing does not stop the clock, and a strict timer will auto-submit when time runs out. You can usually change an answer before you submit, unless your teacher has turned that off for a specific test.

5. After Submitting

If your teacher enabled it, your score appears immediately. Otherwise, wait for your teacher to share results. Where allowed, click Review Answers to see exactly which questions you got right or wrong, with the correct answer shown for each.

6. Notifications

Tap 🔔 Enable notifications on the "My Tests" page to get a phone/browser alert whenever a new test is published — useful once tests are being added daily.

Still stuck?

Contact your teacher directly — they can reset your password or check why a test isn't showing up for your batch.
